Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026 · Operator: NCV — Resume Platform

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what information NCV ("we", "the app") collects, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It applies to the NCV mobile apps for iOS and Android.

2. Information we collect

A. Account information

Your name, email address, mobile number and sign-in details. Passwords are never stored in plain text.

B. Resume and career information

Everything you put in your CV: work experience, education, skills, certifications, achievements, contact details and any CV file you upload. Also your job field, target job title and experience level.

C. Live interview data (Legendary subscription only)

When you start a live interview, the app asks for access to your camera and microphone, and uses them only during the interview.

  • Your voice is streamed in real time to our AI provider so the interviewer can talk with you. The conversation is transcribed so we can show you the transcript and your report.
  • A few still images are taken from your camera during the interview to give feedback on body language, posture and professional appearance. They are processed to produce your report and then deleted. We do not record or keep video of you.
  • We never use these images to identify you, and we do not analyse or comment on physical features, age, gender, skin or weight.
  • The interview report and the list of questions you were asked are kept so that later interviews do not repeat questions.

D. Gmail data (automatic job applications, Legendary subscription only)

If you turn on automatic applications and connect your Google account, NCV asks for one Google permission only: send email on your behalf (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send).

  • We use it only to send the job application emails you turned on, from your own Gmail address, with your CV attached.
  • NCV cannot read, search, delete or change any email in your mailbox, and never asks for that access.
  • The access key Google gives us is stored encrypted on our servers and is never shown in the app.
  • Every email sent for you is listed in the app with its recipient, text and attachments.
  • You can stop automatic applications or disconnect Gmail in the app at any time, or remove NCV's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

E. Automatic applications data

To apply for you, we keep your CV (Arabic and English PDF versions) in private storage that only your account can access, the settings you chose (field, number of applications per day, language), the jobs our search found for you (company, job title, and the public email and link from the job posting), and a log of every application sent.

F. Purchases

Subscriptions are bought through Apple's App Store. We receive confirmation of your subscription (plan and dates) to unlock its features. We never receive or store your card or payment details.

G. Usage and technical information

App settings and preferences, the features you use and how often (for daily usage limits), device type, operating system and app version, a notification token (to send notifications you allowed), and app install attribution information.

3. How we use your information

  • To provide the app's features: building and improving your CV, job matching, sending applications, interview practice and reports.
  • To run automatic applications that you turned on.
  • To manage your account, subscription and usage limits.
  • To send notifications you allowed.
  • To keep the app secure and fix problems.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your data, including Gmail data or interview images, for advertising.

4. Use of Google user data (Limited Use)

NCV's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • Gmail access is used only to send the application emails the user turned on in the app.
  • We do not transfer Google user data to others except as needed to provide this feature, to comply with the law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with notice to the user.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising, and we do not sell it.
  • No person reads Google user data unless the user asks us to for support, it is needed for security, or the law requires it.
  • Google user data is not used to train AI models.

5. Who we share data with

We share data only with the service providers that run the app, and only what they need:

  • Supabase: our database, file storage and server functions.
  • AI providers (OpenAI and Anthropic): to read and improve CVs, write application letters, search for job postings, run the live interview and produce interview reports. Content is sent for processing only.
  • Google: Gmail sending (only if you connect it) and Firebase Cloud Messaging for notifications.
  • Apple: subscription purchases.
  • AppsFlyer: to measure where app installs come from.
  • Employers you apply to: when you send an application, or automatic applications send one for you, the recipient receives your email and CV.
  • Legal requirements: when required by law or a valid court order.

6. How long we keep data

  • Your account, CVs and settings: until you delete them or delete your account.
  • Interview still images: deleted right after the report is produced.
  • Gmail access key: until you disconnect Gmail, remove access in your Google account, or delete your NCV account.
  • Automatic application log and found jobs: until you delete your account.

7. Your rights

  • Access and correct your information in the app.
  • Export your CV as PDF.
  • Turn off automatic applications and disconnect Gmail at any time.
  • Delete your account and its data from Account → Delete Account.
  • Contact us with any privacy question, or to object to a use of your data.

8. Data security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS). Gmail access keys are stored encrypted. CV files are kept in private storage limited to your account. No system is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data.

9. Children's privacy

NCV is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and tell you in the app or by other suitable means.

١. ما نجمعه

  • بيانات الحساب: الاسم والإيميل ورقم الجوال وبيانات الدخول. كلمات المرور لا تُحفظ كنص واضح.
  • بيانات السيرة الذاتية: كل ما تكتبه في سيرتك أو ترفعه، ومجالك والوظيفة المستهدفة ومستوى خبرتك.
  • المقابلة الحية (الاشتراك الأسطوري): نستخدم الكاميرا والمايك أثناء المقابلة فقط. صوتك يُرسل مباشرة لمزوّد الذكاء الاصطناعي حتى يحاورك المحاور، ويُكتب نص المحادثة لتقريرك. نأخذ لقطات قليلة من الكاميرا لتقييم لغة الجسد والجلسة والمظهر المهني، وتُحذف بعد إعداد التقرير. لا نسجّل فيديو ولا نحتفظ به، ولا نعلّق على الملامح أو العمر أو الجنس أو البشرة أو الوزن.
  • Gmail (التقديم التلقائي، الاشتراك الأسطوري): نطلب صلاحية واحدة فقط هي «إرسال الإيميل نيابة عنك»، ونستخدمها فقط لإرسال طلبات التوظيف التي فعّلتها، من إيميلك ومعها سيرتك. لا نستطيع قراءة بريدك أو البحث فيه أو حذفه أو تعديله. مفتاح الوصول محفوظ مشفّراً، وكل إيميل يُرسل يظهر لك في التطبيق. تقدر توقف الخدمة أو تفصل Gmail من التطبيق، أو تلغي الصلاحية من حساب Google.
  • بيانات التقديم التلقائي: سيرتك بالعربي والإنجليزي في تخزين خاص بحسابك، وإعداداتك، والوظائف التي وجدها البحث، وسجل كل تقديم.
  • المشتريات: الاشتراك عبر App Store، ونستلم تأكيد الاشتراك فقط، ولا نستلم بيانات البطاقة.
  • بيانات الاستخدام والجهاز: الإعدادات، والميزات المستخدمة لحساب الحدود اليومية، ونوع الجهاز والنظام، ورمز الإشعارات، ومصدر تثبيت التطبيق.

٢. كيف نستخدمها

لتقديم ميزات التطبيق، وتشغيل التقديم التلقائي الذي فعّلته، وإدارة حسابك واشتراكك، وإرسال الإشعارات التي سمحت بها، وحماية التطبيق. لا نبيع بياناتك، ولا نستخدمها في الإعلانات، ومنها بيانات Gmail ولقطات المقابلة.

٣. بيانات Google

استخدامنا للمعلومات التي نستلمها من واجهات Google يلتزم بسياسة بيانات مستخدمي خدمات Google API، ومنها متطلبات الاستخدام المحدود (Limited Use). لا نستخدم بيانات Google إلا لإرسال طلبات التوظيف التي فعّلتها، ولا نبيعها، ولا نستخدمها في الإعلانات أو في تدريب نماذج الذكاء الاصطناعي.
